 FIELD GUIDE

A little learning.
A lot of unlocked doors.

Each woodland village explores a different cybersecurity topic. Walk with WASD or arrow keys, click the ground, or use the touch arrows. Approach a cottage and press E or Space to knock. You can also use the accessible door list. Restore all eight doors to open the next village. Wrong answers stay in your record; you can try again.

A first-try answer earns 100 XP. Each wrong choice reduces that lock’s reward by 30 XP. Asking Patch for a hint costs 20 possible XP. You always earn at least 10 XP for solving a lock.

Your progress stays here

Your nickname, answers, hints, timestamps, and progress are saved in this browser’s IndexedDB database on this device. No account, password, analytics, or AI service is involved. Nicknames are not private accounts: anyone using this browser can open the same nickname.

Clearing site data can erase progress. A different browser, device, or website address has a separate database. Download your answer history as a JSON backup from the mission map. This version exports backups; it does not import them.

Privacy when you visit

Use a made-up callsign, especially on shared devices. Your callsign and answers are not sent to our server. The hosting provider receives your IP address and technical request data to deliver the site and may process operational or security logs. External reading links open third-party websites with their own privacy practices. An exported answer file contains your callsign and history: keep it private.
